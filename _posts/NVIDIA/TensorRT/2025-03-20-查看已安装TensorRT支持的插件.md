## 查看已安装 TensorRT 支持的插件

```bash
# 按添加顺序
strings /usr/lib/x86_64-linux-gnu/libnvinfer_plugin.so | grep "_TRT"
# 按字母排序
strings /usr/lib/x86_64-linux-gnu/libnvinfer_plugin.so | grep "_TRT" | sort -u
```
